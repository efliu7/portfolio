I’m Ethan, a Software Engineering student at Western University who enjoys turning messy problems into useful, thoughtful products. I’m graduating in June 2027.

Most recently, I spent 16 months at AMD building and modernizing an internal test case management platform used by QA teams around the world. That experience sharpened the way I think about reliable systems, maintainable code, and software that makes someone’s day-to-day work meaningfully easier.

I’m especially interested in full-stack development and practical AI-powered applications, particularly where good engineering can simplify a complicated workflow or make technology feel more intuitive.
